    What people say

    Good questions

    How far ahead should I order?

    Order by 2pm for next-day pickup. For weddings and large orders, please give us two weeks.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    Yes, until 2pm the day before. Reply to your confirmation email and we'll sort it out.

    What if I have allergies?

    Every cake lists its allergens. Our kitchen is nut-free, and gluten-free cakes use a separate prep area.

    How long does a cheesecake keep?

    Five days in the fridge, or two months frozen.
